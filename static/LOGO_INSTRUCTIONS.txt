LOGO FILE INSTRUCTIONS
======================

To add your company logo to the invoice:

1. Place your logo file as "logo.png" in this directory (app/static/)
2. The logo should be:
   - PNG format
   - Recommended size: 200x100 pixels or similar
   - Transparent background preferred
   - High quality for print

3. The logo will appear on the left side of the invoice header
4. It will automatically scale for both screen and print display
